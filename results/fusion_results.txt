Multimodal Fusion Emotion Recognition Results

Model Architecture:
MFCC + BiLSTM speech encoder
DistilBERT text encoder
Late Fusion using concatenation
Dense classifier

Dataset:
Toronto Emotional Speech Set (TESS)

Training Configuration:
- Batch Size: 8
- Epochs: 3
- Optimizer: AdamW
- Learning Rate: 2e-5

Results:
Accuracy: 36.60%

Observation:
The fusion model performed better than the text-only model but worse than the speech-only model.

Reason:
The text modality in TESS contains mostly neutral words repeated across all emotions. Because of this, the text branch adds weak or noisy information to the fusion model.

Conclusion:
Fusion helps compared to text-only classification, but speech-only remains the strongest modality for this dataset.
