Multimodal Fusion Emotion Recognition Results

Model Architecture:
MFCC + BiLSTM speech encoder
DistilBERT text encoder
Late Fusion using concatenation
Dense classifier

Dataset:
Toronto Emotional Speech Set (TESS)

Training Configuration:
- Batch Size: 8
- Epochs: 10
- Optimizer: AdamW
- Learning Rate: 0.001
- Pretrained Speech Encoder Used

Results:
Accuracy: 99.82%

Classification Performance:

- Angry: 99%
- Disgust: 100%
- Fear: 99%
- Happy: 100%
- Neutral: 100%
- Surprise: 100%
- Sad: 100%

Observations:
- Fusion model achieved very high classification accuracy.
- Most emotional classes were perfectly classified.
- Only a very small number of samples were misclassified.

Improvements Applied:
- Loaded pretrained speech encoder weights into fusion model
- Fixed train/test split consistency
- Reduced data leakage risks
- Increased training epochs
- Used stronger fusion training configuration

Analysis:
The speech modality contributed the most useful emotional information. Since the TESS dataset contains emotionally neutral words, the text branch provided limited semantic contribution. The pretrained speech encoder significantly improved the overall fusion performance.

Conclusion:
The multimodal fusion system successfully combined speech and text information for emotion recognition and achieved near-perfect performance on the TESS dataset.
